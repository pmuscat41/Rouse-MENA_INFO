About Rouse in MEA

Patent practice in the Middle East and Africa, since 1997

The Middle East and Africa has shown rapid growth and emerged as a major commercial hub and investment centre.

Rouse established its Middle East and Africa office in Dubai in 1997 and the team has 29 years of patent practice in the region, supported by 14 patent staff across three countries, including three registered patent attorneys.

The practice is consistently ranked Tier 1 / Band 1 for patents by multiple rating agencies — and has been for many years — and is the recipient of multiple industry awards.
