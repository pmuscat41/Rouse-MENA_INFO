### 🗺️ 2. Interactive MEA Map
- **Custom Mapping**: A bespoke interactive map focused on the Middle East and Africa.
- **Visual Feedback**: Real-time highlighting of Rouse-managed countries in brand colors.
- **Seamless Navigation**: Click any country on the map to instantly view its specific IP requirements.

## 📂 Project Structure
- `index.html`: The main landing page and interactive map.
- `country-detail.html`: The dynamic template for all country-specific data.
- `assets/css/main.css`: The core design system and corporate styles.
- `assets/data/countries_data.json`: The structured database for the site.
